EDITOR GUIDE

Pick a tool, then click or drag on the grid. Your level saves as you go (unless auto-save is off in Settings).

Objects

■Block 3 solid ground. Running into its side kills you.
▲Spike 4 kills on touch. Q/E turn it.
◢Slope 5 a ramp for flip and robot; deadly for the wave. Q/E turn it.
●Yellow orb O tap while touching it in mid-air for a small jump (flip, robot).
●Blue orb P tap while touching it in mid-air to flip gravity (flip, robot).
⌫Erase 6 removes anything, including a whole portal column.

Portals (fill a whole column)

FFlip 7 tap to flip gravity.
WWave 8 hold to rise. The row you click is where the wave enters.
RRobot 9 tap to hop, hold to jump higher.
mMini [ / norm ] shrink or restore the wave. Ignored by flip and robot.

Start, checkpoints, goal

S 0 sets the start. ⚑ - adds a checkpoint; click its column again to remove it. G = moves the goal.

Selecting

⬚ 2 click an object or drag a box (Shift adds). Drag the selection to move it. Q/E rotate, Del deletes, Ctrl+C/V/D copy, paste, duplicate, Ctrl+A selects all, Esc clears.

Moving around & testing

✋ 1, right-drag, the scroll wheel or the arrow keys pan the view. test T plays the level; from here starts at the left of the view; check tells you if the level can be beaten and marks where it can't.

Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+Y redo, Ctrl+S save.

If you die upside down as flip or robot, you come back on the ceiling at the checkpoint.

Music

♫ music picks an MP3 for the level. It starts with the level, and after a checkpoint it picks up where the song would be. start at skips the song's intro. The song is kept in this browser; the level JSON only stores its file name.

Sharing

json copies or downloads the level. Use import on My Levels to load one. The format is the same as levels.json, so it also loads in Unity.

Publish
Level music. Pick an MP3 (or any audio file your browser plays). It plays from the start of the level, and after a checkpoint it picks up where the song would be at that point.
Level JSON (the levels.json schema, so Unity can load it). Paste a level or a whole levels.json and press load to add it to My levels.
